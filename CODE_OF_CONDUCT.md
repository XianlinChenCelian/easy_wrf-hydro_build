# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and
welcoming experience, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or
technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and patience.
- Assume that installation questions may come from first-time Linux users.
- Keep bug reports reproducible and remove private information from logs.
- Respect the licenses of WRF-Hydro, Intel software, and all dependencies.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private
information, or intentionally disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The maintainer may
edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior violates this policy.
